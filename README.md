# ELEVEX Beta

Build a premium, modern marketing website for ELEVEX, a digital solutions and technology partner for small and medium-sized businesses. Positioning: not a website agency, not a marketing agency, not an AI consultancy — a business-focused digital solutions partner. Tone: professional, modern, intelligent, confident, approachable, practical, premium without being intimidating. Never use "cutting-edge," "revolutionary," "disruptive," excessive AI jargon, startup clichés, gradients, cartoon illustrations, or generic stock photography.

Brand identity

Wordmark / logo: Typographic only, no icon. Render "ELEVEX" in uppercase, Manrope (or Inter) Extra Bold. Color-split the wordmark: "ELEV" in Cloud White (#F8FAFC or #FFFFFF depending on background), "EX" in Electric Blue (#38BDF8). This is the primary logo across nav, footer, and favicon (favicon can reduce to just a bold "X" in Electric Blue on navy).

Descriptor (used beneath the logo in places like the footer, not the nav): "Digital Solutions Development"

Tagline: "Digital Solutions. Built for Growth."

Brand promise: "We make technology work for your business."

Colors

ELEVEX Navy #0B1220 — primary dark background, navigation

ELEVEX Blue #2563EB — primary accent, CTA buttons

Electric Blue #38BDF8 — secondary accent, the "EX" in the logo, subtle highlights, hover/interactive states

Cloud #F8FAFC — primary light background

Slate #64748B — secondary text

White #FFFFFF — primary light text/background

Dominant palette is navy, white, and blue only — do not use all colors evenly. Never stack more than two accent colors in one section.

Typography

Manrope (preferred) or Inter, loaded via Google Fonts. Headings: Bold/Extra Bold, large, short statements, tight and confident. Body: Regular/Medium, generous line-height (1.6–1.7), short paragraphs — avoid large text blocks.

Layout system

Max content width 1200–1280px, centered, generous side padding

Large section spacing (100–140px vertical padding between major sections on desktop)

Cards: subtle 1px borders, small corner radius (4–6px) — not rounded "SaaS dashboard" cards

Buttons: Primary = blue background (#2563EB), white text, hover = brighter blue (#3B82F6); Secondary = transparent/white background with a subtle border. All primary CTAs end in an arrow: "Book a Consultation →"

Motion: subtle fade-in and slide-up on scroll, hover elevation on cards, smooth number/progress transitions, animated multi-step form transitions. Nothing flashy or excessive.

Overall feeling: modern technology consultancy — not an AI startup landing page.

Hero visual

Do not use a generic computer/AI stock photo or icon. Build an animated network visual as the hero's background or right-hand graphic: a dark navy canvas with a subtle field of connected points (nodes) and thin lines between them, softly drifting/pulsing over time (canvas or SVG + CSS/JS animation, low-key and slow — no fast flashing). A few connections highlight in Electric Blue (#38BDF8) at a time, as if signal is moving through the network, to suggest business systems becoming connected. Keep it restrained and premium, not a busy "matrix" effect. It should read as connection + automation + movement + growth.

Site structure

Navigation

Logo (ELEVEX wordmark, color-split as above) on the left. Nav links: Solutions, How We Work, Why ELEVEX, Contact. Primary CTA button on the right: "Book a Consultation". On mobile, collapse to a clean hamburger menu, keeping "Book a Consultation" visible/accessible at all times (e.g., sticky in the mobile menu header).

7. Multi-step "Book a Consultation" flow

Build this as a mobile-first, single-question-per-step interactive form with a visible progress indicator, back navigation, and preserved answers between steps. Use large, tappable answer cards (not plain radio buttons). Validate inline. Subtle slide/fade transition between steps. This should feel like "let's figure out how we can help," not a corporate application.

Step 1 — What do you need? (multi-select cards) Headline: "What can we help you improve?" / Supporting text: "Select the areas you're interested in." Options: Website & Digital Presence ("I need a new website or want to improve my current one."), Automation ("I want to reduce manual work or automate processes."), AI ("I want to explore practical AI solutions for my business."), Lead Generation ("I want to generate, capture or manage more leads."), Integrations ("I need my systems and tools to work better together."), Not Sure ("I know something needs to improve, but I'm not sure what solution I need.") Button: "Continue →"

Step 2 — Biggest challenge (single-select cards) Headline: "What's your biggest challenge right now?" Options: Too much manual work, Not enough leads, Poor digital presence, Disconnected systems, Slow processes, We want to use AI, Something else (each with the one-line description from the spec). Button: "Continue →"

Step 3 — Timeline (single-select cards) Headline: "When are you looking to make a change?" Options: As soon as possible, Within 1–3 months, Within 3–6 months, Just exploring. Button: "Continue →"

Step 4 — Contact details (form fields) Headline: "Let's continue the conversation." / Supporting text: "Leave your details and we'll get back to you to discuss your goals and potential next steps." Fields: First Name, Last Name, Business/Company, Work Email, Phone, Website (optional), and an optional message field with placeholder "Tell us briefly about your business or what you're trying to achieve." CTA: "Request a Consultation →" Privacy microcopy beneath the form: "By submitting this form, you agree to be contacted by ELEVEX regarding your inquiry."

Success screen (after submission) Headline: "Thanks. We've got it." Copy: "We've received your request and will review the information you provided. The next step is a short conversation about your business, your goals and where ELEVEX may be able to help." CTA: "Book Your Consultation →" with a secondary "I'll schedule later" link. Leave a placeholder container where a calendar/scheduler embed can be added later.

8. Final CTA (dark navy full-width section)

Eyebrow: READY TO MOVE FORWARD?

Headline: "Let's build something better."

Copy: "Whether you're looking to modernize your digital presence, automate your operations, implement AI or create a better path to growth, ELEVEX can help you find the right place to start."

Primary CTA: "Book a Consultation →" / Secondary CTA: "Tell Us About Your Business"

9. Footer

Logo + descriptor "Digital Solutions Development" + one-line tagline "Digital solutions built for growing businesses." Columns: Navigation (Solutions, How We Work, Why ELEVEX, Contact), Solutions (Digital Presence, Automation, AI Solutions, Growth Systems), Contact (email placeholder). Footer CTA: "Book a Consultation →" Bottom line: "© 2026 ELEVEX. All rights reserved." with Privacy Policy / Terms of Service links.

CTA rules (apply site-wide)

Use "Book a Consultation" as the consistent primary conversion action everywhere. Never use generic CTAs like "Get Started," "Submit," "Contact Us," or "Learn More." Acceptable CTA variety elsewhere on the page: "Start a Conversation," "Explore Solutions," "See How We Can Help," "Talk to ELEVEX."

SEO

Page title: "ELEVEX | Digital Solutions Development for Growing Businesses" Meta description: "ELEVEX helps growing businesses build better digital experiences, automate processes, implement practical AI solutions and connect the systems they rely on." Do not keyword-stuff.

Technical notes

Fully responsive, mobile-first

Smooth scroll for in-page nav anchors

Accessible: proper heading hierarchy, sufficient contrast, keyboard-navigable multi-step form

Use Manrope from Google Fonts for headings and body

Build the multi-step consultation flow as reusable components (progress bar, answer card, step container) so steps can be reordered or extended later
